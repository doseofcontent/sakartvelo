# Sakartvelo Skoniai, UAB - Design Style Guide

## Design Philosophy

### Cultural Heritage Meets Modern Elegance
The design draws inspiration from Georgia's 8,000-year winemaking tradition, blending ancient cultural elements with contemporary sophistication. The aesthetic evokes the warmth of Georgian hospitality, the richness of wine culture, and the authenticity of traditional craftsmanship.

### Visual Language
- **Warmth & Hospitality**: Earthy, inviting tones that reflect Georgian landscapes and wine culture
- **Authenticity & Tradition**: Elements that honor 8,000 years of winemaking heritage
- **Sophistication & Quality**: Clean, modern presentation that conveys premium quality
- **Cultural Richness**: Subtle Georgian motifs and patterns integrated thoughtfully

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Deep Wine Red**: #722F37 (Primary brand color, inspired by Saperavi wine)
- **Warm Terracotta**: #B85450 (Secondary accent, reflecting Qvevri clay)
- **Golden Amber**: #D4A574 (Highlight color, representing amber wines)

### Neutral Base
- **Cream White**: #F8F6F0 (Background, like aged parchment)
- **Warm Gray**: #6B6B6B (Text, balanced and readable)
- **Charcoal**: #2C2C2C (Dark text and accents)

### Supporting Colors
- **Sage Green**: #8B9A7A (Natural accent, representing Georgian landscapes)
- **Warm Beige**: #E8DCC0 (Section backgrounds and cards)

## Typography

### Primary Font: Playfair Display (Serif)
- **Usage**: Headings, hero text, wine names
- **Character**: Elegant, sophisticated, with classical proportions
- **Inspiration**: Traditional wine labels and premium branding

### Secondary Font: Inter (Sans-serif)
- **Usage**: Body text, navigation, forms, descriptions
- **Character**: Clean, modern, highly readable
- **Purpose**: Ensures excellent readability across all devices

### Font Hierarchy
- **H1**: Playfair Display, 3.5rem, Deep Wine Red
- **H2**: Playfair Display, 2.5rem, Charcoal
- **H3**: Playfair Display, 1.8rem, Warm Terracotta
- **Body**: Inter, 1rem, Warm Gray
- **Small Text**: Inter, 0.875rem, Warm Gray

## Visual Effects & Animations

### Core Libraries Used
1. **Anime.js**: Smooth entrance animations, wine card hover effects
2. **ECharts.js**: Wine region data visualization, timeline graphics
3. **Splide.js**: Hero image carousel, wine showcase sliders
4. **p5.js**: Interactive Georgian wine map, particle effects
5. **Pixi.js**: Subtle background textures and wine-like fluid effects
6. **Matter.js**: Playful wine drop physics on loading screens
7. **Shader-park**: Warm, wine-colored background gradients

### Animation Style
- **Gentle & Organic**: Movements inspired by wine pouring and natural flow
- **Warm Color Transitions**: Subtle shifts between wine-inspired hues
- **Elegant Timing**: Smooth, unhurried animations that reflect premium quality
- **Cultural Motifs**: Subtle Georgian patterns in loading animations

### Header Effects
- **Gradient Flow**: Warm wine-colored gradients that subtly shift
- **Parallax Elements**: Gentle movement of decorative Georgian elements
- **Text Animations**: Staggered letter reveals for headings

### Interactive Elements
- **Wine Card Hover**: 3D tilt effect with warm shadow expansion
- **Button Hover**: Color morphing from terracotta to wine red
- **Image Galleries**: Ken Burns effect with warm overlay transitions
- **Form Focus**: Gentle glow effects in amber tones

## Layout & Composition

### Grid System
- **Desktop**: 12-column grid with generous whitespace
- **Tablet**: 8-column grid with adapted spacing
- **Mobile**: 4-column grid with touch-friendly elements

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Generous Whitespace**: Reflects premium positioning and elegance
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Consistent spacing based on 24px baseline
- **Content Hierarchy**: Clear visual separation between sections

### Section Design
- **Hero Sections**: Full-width with centered content and subtle overlays
- **Content Blocks**: Alternating layouts with wine-inspired backgrounds
- **Cards & Panels**: Soft shadows with warm, terracotta-tinted borders

## Cultural Integration

### Georgian Elements
- **Subtle Patterns**: Traditional Georgian motifs as background textures
- **Wine Imagery**: Authentic photography of Qvevri, vineyards, and wine
- **Cultural Symbols**: Tasteful integration of Georgian cultural elements
- **Color Harmony**: All cultural elements harmonized with wine-inspired palette

### Authenticity Markers
- **Qvevri Imagery**: Clay vessel textures and shapes in design elements
- **Vineyard Landscapes**: Background imagery from Georgian wine regions
- **Traditional Typography**: Respectful use of Georgian script where appropriate
- **Cultural Storytelling**: Design elements that support the narrative of Georgian wine heritage

## Responsive Design Principles

### Mobile-First Approach
- **Touch-Friendly**: All interactive elements sized for finger navigation
- **Readable Text**: Font sizes optimized for mobile reading
- **Simplified Navigation**: Collapsible menu with clear hierarchy
- **Optimized Images**: Responsive imagery that maintains quality

### Desktop Enhancement
- **Rich Interactions**: Hover effects and detailed animations
- **Expanded Content**: Additional information and larger imagery
- **Multi-Column Layouts**: Efficient use of screen real estate
- **Advanced Features**: Enhanced filtering and search capabilities

This design system creates a cohesive, culturally rich, and visually stunning experience that honors Georgian wine tradition while providing modern usability and elegance.
